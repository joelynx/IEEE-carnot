# Supplementary material

`supplement.pdf` (source: `supplement.tex`) is supplementary material to the Working Notes paper, by the same author. It is not part of the paper submitted to the conference. It keeps three sections of the paper in full as they stood in the version submitted for review, and adds the derivation of the split of wrong final answers and the full results of the arithmetic replay.
